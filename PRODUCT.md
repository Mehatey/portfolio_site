# Product context

## What this site is

Siddharth Mehta’s portfolio is a hiring focused personal website for product design roles, with a secondary signal of creative technology, spatial computing, AI prototyping, and playful visual experimentation.

The site should help a recruiter or hiring manager understand three things quickly:

1. Sid can design clear digital products.
2. Sid can build and prototype technically ambitious experiences.
3. Sid has a distinct creative voice, not a generic portfolio template.

## Primary audience

- Product design recruiters
- Design hiring managers
- Creative technology leads
- Founders or teams looking for a designer who can work through ambiguity

Most visitors will skim. The site must support a fast path before asking anyone to explore long visual archives.

## Priority role

Product design is the priority route.

The work order and first impression should favor product design credibility first, then spatial and experimental work as a differentiator.

Recommended recruiter path:

1. Home
2. Works
3. Mool
4. AI Prototypes
5. Encoded
6. About
7. Contact

## Core pages

### Home

Purpose: establish Sid’s identity and proof quickly while preserving the atmospheric cube and desk world.

The home page should feel cinematic, personal, and strange in a controlled way. It should not be so cryptic that visitors miss what Sid does.

### Works

Purpose: let recruiters compare projects and choose quickly.

The works page should prioritize project name, discipline, outcome, role, and proof within the first view. Visual previews are important, but they should not obscure the decision path.

### Project details

Purpose: tell a compact case story first, then offer deeper visual proof.

Every case should open with:

1. Project identity
2. Scope
3. Role
4. Proof or outcome
5. Short overview
6. Key metrics
7. Visual story

Avoid making recruiters scroll through large archives before they understand the project.

### Play

Purpose: show taste, curiosity, and creative range without diluting the product design narrative.

The default Play mode is curated experiments. Camera roll content lives in a separate mode so photos and videos do not overwhelm the creative technology story.

### About

Purpose: make Sid feel credible, human, and easy to place on a team.

Keep the page personal and polished. It should not become a second resume before the visitor understands the person.

### Contact

Purpose: make the hiring path obvious.

Email, LinkedIn, resume, phone, and location should remain easy to find. Decorative motion should support calm confidence, not compete with the contact actions.

## Content principles

- Lead with specific proof over vague adjectives.
- Prefer short case summaries before long galleries.
- Avoid AI sounding filler and generic claims.
- Avoid dashes in user facing copy when a comma or sentence break works.
- Keep labels useful. Tiny uppercase labels should be used sparingly.
- Use direct recruiter language where it helps clarity.

## Interaction principles

- Motion should feel intentional, interruptible, and calm.
- No hover only primary navigation.
- No scroll trapping.
- Reduced motion support is required.
- Icons and Easter eggs should add personality without blocking comprehension.
- Effects should never make text harder to read.

## Technical context

- Static Jekyll site based on al-folio.
- Main custom UI lives in `_layouts`, `_includes`, `play/index.html`, and project asset folders.
- GitHub Pages serves `siddharthmehta.design`.
- Heavy visual work must be balanced against deploy size and page performance.
