Reflection

Three days to understand the stock market and turn it into a game. I had to start teaching it before I’d finished learning it myself. Simplification is not dumbing down: every financial concept has a core mechanic, and finding that mechanic was the real design problem. Pixel art made commitment unavoidable. Each shape landed and stayed.
