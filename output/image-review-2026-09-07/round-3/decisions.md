## QA and remaining limits

All nine composites inspected for hands, device geometry, screen fit, typography, masking edges and credible depth of field. Original typography and logos are inserted from source pixels, with perspective resampling and subtle lighting adjustments. Original authoring errors remain original rather than being silently rewritten. Generated keyboard legends and small environmental details are approximate.

Gallery checked at 1440px and 390px: all 45 images loaded, three videos decoded with no media errors, no horizontal overflow. Desktop comparisons display at 678 × 452; mobile at 354 × 236. Actual case-study images display at 1200 × 800 desktop and 346 × 231 mobile. The setting remains understandable on mobile, while dense UI requires opening the source or full-size image.

Placement screenshots, motion contact sheets, source copies, prompts, raw generations, masks and compositing scripts are saved in this folder. `sources.json` lists provenance and reproduction commands. `qa.json` records the final browser checks. Previous rounds remain available.
