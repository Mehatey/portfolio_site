The whole footer is water: the pond, the music panel over it, the wall sitting in it. So the only thing below the water that means anything is coming up out of it. This band is that — deep teal at the bottom of the scroll, turquoise through the middle, and a near-white light at the top, swept as one diagonal so it reads as a surface passing rather than a colour fading. It comes back down before the colophon. An earlier version ran the white all the way to the bottom, which looks better in a screenshot and breaks the footer: the colophon and the sign-off are light ink, and they simply vanished. The band peaks and returns, so the page ends on its own ground and nothing below has to be re-inked. Everything is one custom property. --q is 0 as the band enters and 1 when it has crossed, written by the observer below; the gradient stops, the caustic drift and the light bloom all read off it, so there is one clock and no chance of two of them disagreeing. {% endcomment %}
