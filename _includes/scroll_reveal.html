{%- comment -%} Site-wide scroll reveal. Content rises and sharpens from a soft blur as it enters the viewport, mirroring the About/Contact motion language so the whole site shares one vocabulary. Opt in by adding `data-reveal` to an element; direct siblings stagger automatically. Robust by design: an IntersectionObserver animates elements in on normal scroll, and a rAF-throttled scroll sweep reveals anything the observer skips during a fast scroll or an anchor jump. With reduced-motion, no observer, or any error, everything is shown immediately. Content is never stuck hidden. {%- endcomment -%}
