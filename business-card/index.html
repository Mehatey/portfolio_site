How to print

Spec: 3.5" × 2" single-sided. Solid black (#08080c) on 16pt soft-touch matte cardstock. Add 0.125" bleed if your printer asks (browser PDF export is cut-line exact).

Spacing applied (Swiss-grid principles): 0.3" outer padding all four sides. Identity group sits tight at top-left (8–14px micro-gaps inside). QR small at 0.7" bottom-right. Diagonal balance: identity TL ↔ utility BR. Empty middle is intentional — premium cards earn their negative space.

QR test: Scan with your phone (the preview here is 2× size, plenty of resolution). Should open siddharthmehta.design.

Export: Cmd+P → "Save as PDF". The preview-scale transform auto-resets in print mode so the PDF comes out at exact 3.5"×2".
