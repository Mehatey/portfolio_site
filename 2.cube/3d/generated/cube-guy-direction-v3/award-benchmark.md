## What the winning work actually does

1. One governing metaphor. KOKUYO uses pop-up construction. Monolith makes touch rewrite reality. Symphony of Vines uses one cinematic journey. Effects never feel imported from another project.
2. State changes carry meaning. Scroll does not simply rotate a model. It changes the character's condition, the camera's role, and what the visitor understands.
3. Strong silhouette before shaders. Hero objects remain readable in every frame. Particles and distortion support the focal subject instead of covering it.
4. Authored camera language. Close-up, reveal, crossing, wide environment, transformation, return. Not one locked camera with random parallax.
5. Interaction has consequence. Pointer response exposes personality or changes the world. It is not ambient noise.
6. Restraint. One hero, one transition language, one accent effect at a time.
7. Performance is designed into the visual system. Compressed assets, progressive loading, controlled DPR, and lazy scene activation make heavy work feel smooth.

## V2 rejection

- Generic mannequin silhouette
- Scans selected by availability, not narrative need
- Foreground geometry obscuring the hero
- Panorama used as wallpaper instead of spatial composition
- Particle quantity mistaken for craft
- Scroll clips without causal storytelling
- Dissolve represented by unrelated white fragments
- Camera and character motion competing simultaneously

## V3 story

1. Mark: flat watercolor square on physical paper. Cursor creates a small pressure dent.
2. Fold: paper corners lift. Square gains thickness. Stitches draw themselves around the cube.
3. Body: thread descends from the cube and sews the body together. First blink. First eye contact.
4. Crossing: he tears the paper plane and steps through it. Camera crosses the tear after him.
5. Work: footsteps grow small portfolio artifacts. Each artifact maps to a real project and remains clickable.
6. Unmake: pointer gently attracts pigment from one side of his body. Silhouette stays readable. Releasing pointer lets him recover.
7. Gift: he reassembles, returns to the browser plane, and offers a small geometric idea. Clicking it opens selected work.

## Production rule

Do not resume homepage coding with the old GLB. First model and rig the approved V3 character sheet. Then create three quality-gate renders: flat-to-volume, walking profile, and controlled point dissolve. Only after those frames match the storyboard should the WebGPU scroll implementation begin.
